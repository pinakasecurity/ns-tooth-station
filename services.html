Dental services

Services listed by the clinic.

General information only. A registered dentist must assess the patient before making a diagnosis or recommending treatment.

Dental Implants

An implant replaces the root of a missing tooth with a titanium post, which supports a crown. Suitability depends on bone volume, gum health and general health, and is assessed before planning. The stages, timeline and fees are explained after that assessment.

Illustrative visual only. Treatment suitability depends on individual clinical assessment.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners move teeth gradually using a series of removable trays. The clinic offers Invisalign, Illusion and DentCare, and the system is chosen to suit the case. Suitability, duration and fees are confirmed after an examination.

Illustrative visual only. Treatment suitability depends on individual clinical assessment.

Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment removes infected tissue from inside the tooth and seals it, so the natural tooth can be kept. It is usually followed by a filling or a crown. The diagnosis and the number of visits are confirmed after examination.

Illustrative visual only. Treatment suitability depends on individual clinical assessment.

Biomimetic Dentistry

A tooth-restoration approach that aims to preserve and replicate natural tooth structure, strength and function. It is used in restorative procedures such as fillings, depending on the condition of the tooth. The aim is to keep as much natural tooth as the case allows.

Illustrative visual only. Treatment suitability depends on individual clinical assessment.

Crowns, Bridges & Dentures

A crown covers a damaged or weakened tooth, a bridge replaces one or more missing teeth, and a denture replaces a section or a full arch. The choice depends on the teeth remaining and on the bite. Impressions or scans and a review appointment are usually involved.

Illustrative visual only. Treatment suitability depends on individual clinical assessment.

Braces & Aligners

Fixed braces and removable aligners both straighten teeth, and the choice depends on the case, age and preference. Treatment usually involves review appointments over several months. Duration and fees are confirmed after assessment.

Illustrative visual only. Treatment suitability depends on individual clinical assessment.

General & Family Dentistry

Routine care covers check-ups, cleaning, gum care and extractions, along with children's dentistry. These visits are also where problems are found early. Appointments are recommended.

Illustrative visual only. Treatment suitability depends on individual clinical assessment.
